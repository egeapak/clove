Queries run on pooled read connections (cloved/src/readers.rs). The index mutex is held only for the refresh and to take a connection, not for the SQL. A reindex takes the pool gate exclusively. The per-query COUNT(*) and directory scan are gone: a refresh with nothing reported is one stat. The total and the rows of one answer are no longer read under one lock, so a write between the two can make total differ from the rows by that write.
