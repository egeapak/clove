mkprompt.py asks the agent to also write its final reply to reply.txt next to the toy project. score.py adds id_titles: for every id in the reply that exists in the store, the item title (ignoring backticks, quotes, case, and spacing) must start within 12 characters after the id. The score records reply_ids (ids named); a reply with none skips the check. Verified on hand-written replies for one s5 store: all titled 1.0, bare ids 0.0, one of two titled 0.5, no ids skipped.
