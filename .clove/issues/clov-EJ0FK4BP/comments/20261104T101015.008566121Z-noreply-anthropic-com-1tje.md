Root cause: ensure_daemon_at returned at once on ClientError::Timeout. A hub that has bound hub.sock but has not reached its accept loop yet (still starting, on a loaded machine) takes the connection and leaves the handshake unanswered past ANSWER_TIMEOUT (500 ms), so the start failed with 'Timeout' instead of retrying. Fix: crates/clove-ipc/src/spawn.rs treats Timeout like Connect/Transport and retries until READY_TIMEOUT (10 s). New test knob CLOVED_SERVE_DELAY_MS (lifecycle.rs: sleep after bind, before serving) and test crates/cloved/tests/daemon_race.rs::a_start_waits_for_a_hub_that_has_bound_but_does_not_serve_yet.
